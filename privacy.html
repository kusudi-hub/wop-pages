Privacy

What we collect, why we collect it, and how to get rid of it.

Last updated November 12, 2026

This covers Alignment and the pages at join.worldofpurpose.com. It is written to be read, not to be impressive. If anything here is unclear, ask and we will explain it in plain words.

What we collect

  • Your name, email and phone number, because we need a way to give you access and reach you.
  • What you give each month, so we can show it back to you and so you can change it.
  • What you write in a scholarship application, if you apply for one.
  • Your purpose declaration, if you choose to write one.
  • Basic activity, like whether an email was opened or a page was visited.

What we never see

Your card details. Payments are handled by Whop. Card numbers go straight to them and never touch World of Purpose systems. When you update your card you are doing it on Whop, not here.

Why we collect it

To give you access to what you paid for, to send you the things that come with membership, to answer you when you write in, and to keep the lights on. That is it.

If you applied for a scholarship, what you wrote is used to make that decision and to check in with you kindly later. It is not shared outside the people who run World of Purpose.

Who else touches it

We use a small number of tools to run this, and your information passes through them:

  • Whop for payments and billing.
  • GoHighLevel for contact records and email.
  • Skool for the community itself.
  • Make to connect those pieces together.

Each of those has its own privacy terms. We only send them what they need to do their job.

What we do not do

We do not sell your information. We do not rent it, trade it, or hand it to advertisers. We do not publish what you wrote in a scholarship application.

Getting your information out, or deleted

Write to kusudi@worldofpurpose.com and ask. We will send you what we hold about you, or delete it, whichever you want. Deleting it means ending your membership too, because we cannot run the membership without it.

Some records have to be kept for tax and accounting reasons even after you leave. Those are payment records, not the things you wrote.

Email

Every email we send has an unsubscribe link, except the ones we have to send about your own account, like a receipt or a failed payment. You can stop the rest at any time without losing access.

Children

Alignment is for adults. It is not built for anyone under 18 and we do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has signed up, tell us and we will remove it.

Changes

If this changes in a way that matters, we will say so rather than quietly updating the date at the top.

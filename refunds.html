Refunds and cancellation

Short version. If it is not for you, ask and you get your money back. No call, no back and forth.

Last updated November 12, 2026

Ask within 7 days of your first payment and we send that payment back. One email to kusudi@worldofpurpose.com. You do not have to explain yourself and nobody will try to talk you out of it.

How billing works

Alignment is pay what you can. You pick your amount when you join, anywhere from $5.11 to $511, and that amount is charged every 30 days. It is every 30 days rather than the same date each month, so your charge date can drift slightly over time.

Every amount gets the same thing. Nobody gets a better version of Alignment for paying more.

Changing what you give

You can lower your amount at any time from your membership page, and the new amount applies from your next charge. If you want to give more, you can start a new membership at the higher amount from the join page.

Stopping

You can stop your membership at any time from your membership page. You keep full access until the end of the period you have already paid for, and nothing is charged after that. There is no cancellation fee and no notice period.

If you change your mind after stopping, you can pick your membership back up from the same page.

The 7 day refund

If you ask within 7 days of your first payment, we refund that payment in full. Write to kusudi@worldofpurpose.com from the email address on your membership and say you want a refund. That is the whole process.

After those first 7 days we do not refund payments that have already been taken, because you had access for the time you paid for. You can still stop at any time so that nothing further is charged.

Scholarships

If you are on a free scholarship there is nothing to refund and nothing to cancel. No card is ever charged. If you want to stop being a member, write to us and we will take care of it.

If a payment fails

Cards expire and banks decline things. If a payment fails we will let you know and give you a chance to update your card before anything changes about your access. You can update your card from your membership page at any time.

Who takes the payment

Payments are processed by Whop. Your card details go directly to them and are never held by World of Purpose. Your statement will show WHOP*WORLDOFPURPOSE.
